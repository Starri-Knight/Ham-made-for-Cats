    If you have so much free time that you got through my whole website and looked through it all, and you’re still so, SO bored, with NOTHING to do with your free time…

    …then do this instead:

    Go play Freerice!

    Freerice is a free trivia game run by the UN World Food Programme. Every question you get right raises 10 grains of rice, and the game’s sponsors turn those grains into real donations to the World Food Programme, with no ads and no account needed.

    645 million people faced hunger in 2025, roughly 1 in every 13 people on Earth, and many of them are children. The World Food Programme gets food to families caught in emergencies, conflict and disasters, and every grain you earn helps it get there.

    Ten right answers is a hundred grains of rice. Being bored has never done so much good.

    Play Freerice play.freerice.com

    And when you’re done saving the world, check out my buddy Bin’s website. It’s a great inspiration for mine: bin.moe
